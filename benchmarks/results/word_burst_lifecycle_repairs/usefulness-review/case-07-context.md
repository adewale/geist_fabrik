# Exhibition plan

The harbour archive opened and the project is complete. The registrar owns recordings, permissions and later withdrawal requests. A speaker can request removal through the museum contact address; the duty curator can remove an extract while a longer review takes place. Keep the permission table with the recording, not just beside a panel layout that may later change.

This page is now an archive of the project. Do not copy its completed tasks into next month's plan. The useful unfinished responsibility belongs to named people in the museum, not to the original volunteer group. [[Listening]] records the approach to speakers and [[Field methods]] records selection limits. The project's smaller presence in my working notes is the intended consequence of a successful handover.
