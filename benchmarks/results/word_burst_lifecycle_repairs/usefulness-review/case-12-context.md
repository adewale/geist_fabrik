# A visit without coordinating anything

Went to Mum's after work and listened to her describe the choir's new conductor. I did not ask about the callback or the tablets. Everything urgent was already handled. [[Care arrangements]] exists to make this sort of visit possible. There is no useful measure of the conversation that would improve it by becoming another line on the checklist.

# Care arrangements

Mum chooses her appointments and keeps the current question card. Lena holds the spare key by agreement, with a backup for holidays. Offers of transport have an end date. Urgent changes need a call; ordinary updates can wait until evening.

Keep current contacts and the next review on half a page. Old letters stay behind the current plan, marked when superseded. The callback window request and accessible route are available if Mum wants them. [[Routes]] holds the journey details. This notebook should make company possible without turning every visit into a coordination meeting.

# Who waters during the half-term trip

Asked two neighbours to water different beds and drew a map of the shut-off taps. Each assumed the other would do the pots. Label responsibility by place rather than by the word garden. The plants at greatest risk are also the least visible from the gate. [[Garden notebook]] should say what can safely be left dry instead of making every brown leaf an emergency.

# Harbour archive labels in two languages

The harbour archive label draft gives twice as much space to the English text. The translation has been shortened until its verbs disappear. Rather than make the type smaller, remove one photograph and keep both accounts legible. A display budget is also a decision about who has to work harder. [[Exhibition plan]] needs a physical mock-up before another meeting about inclusivity.
