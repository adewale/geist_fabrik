# Volunteer handbook

Open the shutter, inspect the floor and check that the exit path is clear. The key card stays in the secure box. Compare the room with the dated photograph beside the checklist; a new water stain deserves a question even when no alarm sounds. A damaged tool goes in the red tray with a description and a review date.

Compliance training covers safety rules, while supervised practice covers tool return and visitor arrival. A person can pause a task when uncertain. Rafi and Jo share closing, including the unlisted tidying time. [[Maintenance]] holds equipment decisions. This page describes an ordinary shift and the routes for getting help; it does not certify anyone to diagnose an electrical fault.

# Exhibition plan

The harbour archive opened and the project is complete. The registrar owns recordings, permissions and later withdrawal requests. A speaker can request removal through the museum contact address; the duty curator can remove an extract while a longer review takes place. Keep the permission table with the recording, not just beside a panel layout that may later change.

This page is now an archive of the project. Do not copy its completed tasks into next month's plan. The useful unfinished responsibility belongs to named people in the museum, not to the original volunteer group. [[Listening]] records the approach to speakers and [[Field methods]] records selection limits. The project's smaller presence in my working notes is the intended consequence of a successful handover.

# Pottery

Working notes from the Tuesday class. Clay remembers uneven thickness during drying and firing; my hands do not reliably notice it while throwing. Photograph the section of a broken pot before sweeping it away. Record glaze combinations on the base of a test tile and keep the tile beside the recipe.

I prefer handles that look spare, but a handle is also a thing somebody must grip while the cup is hot and heavy. Test with water and with different hands. The class cupboard makes it possible to work with very few personal tools. Do not confuse that shared support with proof that nobody needs equipment. [[Maintenance]] holds the repair and failure notes. This page should remain a place to return to making, not a list of objects I owe the world.
