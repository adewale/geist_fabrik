# A useful repair we did not complete

The lamp needs a switch we cannot source locally. At the repair cafe we labelled the part and wrote the order code, then returned it safely disassembled. The owner was pleased to know what remained. [[Maintenance]]: unfinished is not the same as abandoned when the next action is clear and chosen by the person who owns the object.

# A small service with a clear edge

The repair cafe is more useful when it can explain what it doesn't do. Clear limits protect visitors from waiting for impossible repairs and volunteers from improvising unsafe promises. [[Maintenance]] should include the edge of our responsibility. A generous invitation does not require an unlimited obligation.

# A measure that ignores the refusal

The repair cafe spreadsheet counts repairs, so a careful refusal looks like failure. Add a category for safe diagnosis and explain it to visitors. Otherwise the metric will quietly reward attempting jobs beyond our tools or competence. [[Field methods]] needs examples where a good decision produces less visible output, not a bigger number.

# Field methods

Write down the situation before explaining it. Who was present, where did I stand, what was asked, and who could not take part? Separate a direct observation from a person's account and from my interpretation. A notebook can preserve all three without pretending they have equal certainty.

Useful comparisons name the denominator and the conditions. A photograph of a busy table does not establish how often the table is busy. An interview selected through friends does not represent everybody who lives nearby. Keep exclusions visible and preserve the cases that did not fit the first story. [[Listening]] is a companion practice: leave room for the person to disagree with the account. Small practical decisions still need evidence, but they do not all need a grand claim about the world.
