# Volunteer handbook

Open the shutter, inspect the floor and check that the exit path is clear. The key card stays in the secure box. Compare the room with the dated photograph beside the checklist; a new water stain deserves a question even when no alarm sounds. A damaged tool goes in the red tray with a description and a review date.

Compliance training covers safety rules, while supervised practice covers tool return and visitor arrival. A person can pause a task when uncertain. Rafi and Jo share closing, including the unlisted tidying time. [[Maintenance]] holds equipment decisions. This page describes an ordinary shift and the routes for getting help; it does not certify anyone to diagnose an electrical fault.
